Questions, answered

When do I start opening it?
Door number 1 opens on the 1st of December, then one door a day until Christmas Eve. It is a countdown, so it is best in their hands before the 1st.
Will it arrive before December?
Orders leave the warehouse in 1 to 3 business days and arrive 7 to 14 business days after that, with free tracking. Order by 10 November to open door number 1 on the 1st. Cutting it close? Message us through our contact page before you order and we will tell you honestly.
Do you ship to my country?
Yes. Shipping is free and tracked worldwide. Depending on where you live, your country may charge import taxes on arrival.
What's inside?
24 flat acrylic charms printed in full colour, each about 4 cm (1.6 in) with a little loop to hang it: six vintage cameras (a box camera, a twin-lens reflex, a rangefinder, a folding bellows camera, a compact and an instant camera) plus a lens, a light meter, a film canister, a roll of film, a stack of prints, a negative strip, a tripod, a flash, a flash bulb, a camera bag, a strap, a cable release, a framed photo, a slide, a loupe, a darkroom timer, prints pegged on a line and an instant print. They are flat printed charms for the tree, a bag or a desk, not 3D models or working cameras.
Can I buy more than one?
Yes, and each one gets cheaper: $35.99 each for two, $33.99 each for three and $31.99 each for four. One for the photographer, one for the friend who borrows their camera.
What if it arrives damaged?
Message us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo, and we replace it or refund it, free. In most cases you will not need to send it back.
Can I return it?
Unopened calendars can come back within 30 days of delivery for any reason. Return shipping on a change of mind is paid by you. See our refund policy for the details.
